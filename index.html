Repair or replace?

You may not need a new windshield at all

A repair is cheaper, quicker and keeps the factory seal. Here is roughly how it splits — send us a photo and we'll tell you honestly which one you need.

Usually repairable

Chips and short cracks

  • Chip smaller than a quarter
  • Crack shorter than about six inches
  • Damage outside the driver's line of sight
  • Nothing through to the inner layer
Needs replacing

Long or deep damage

  • Cracks reaching the edge of the glass
  • Damage right in front of the driver
  • Several chips across the screen
  • Anything through both layers of glass
